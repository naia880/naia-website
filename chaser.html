The Chaser

Your Gateway to Hidden Horizons.

The Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin enhances your charter experience with NAIA by offering unparalleled exploration capabilities. The addition of this high-speed chase boat enables guests to discover secluded bays and hidden gems, offering a smooth, comfortable ride with stabilisers at anchor and underway.

Its design integrates open and enclosed boating, featuring a fully enclosed, weatherproof cabin with a distinctive look that challenges traditional cabin boats. Key features such as the innovative Gullwing Door concept, a spacious foredeck, and a versatile aft deck, complemented by significant gains in fuel economy and performance, ensure a comfortable and efficient journey.
