Recognition

Awards

A design recognised by the world.

NAIA has garnered recognition in the yachting world, having been awarded two prestigious awards in 2009. This is a result of her exceptional design by renowned Francesco Paszkowski, whose work is known for blending performance with style, making NAIA an exemplary model of his design philosophy.

01
2009 · Winner

Best Exterior Styling

Awarded across all classes of motor yachts. The jury recognised NAIA's sculptural confidence — a profile of restraint, proportion, and quiet authority that has aged without compromise.

02
2009 · Winner

Best Semi Displacement

Awarded in the 30–39 metre class. Performance and poise are inseparable — a hull that crosses oceans with the same composure it holds in harbour.

03
2025 · Nominee

Refitted Yachts · 499GT and Below

Nominated in the Refitted Yachts 499GT and below class. Renewal and refinement — a yacht reborn to deliver modern performance while preserving the composure of her original design.

04
2026 · Nominee

Refit Award

Recognised in the Refit category. NAIA's recent refit, delivered by Seara Maritime, introduces a thoughtful upgrade to both form and function — bringing renewed comfort and technical precision throughout.
